# Profile: skills (music, electronics, drawing, cooking, hands-on work)

## Practice

- **Deliberate practice:** short sessions with one clear goal, immediate feedback and gradual difficulty.
- Each chapter has a **practice routine** with minutes and measurable targets: warm-up, technique, the chapter's new skill, application (a song, a circuit, a drawing, a recipe).
- **Done criterion** that the reader can check alone: "change G → C in 4 beats at 80 bpm, 8 times without stopping"; "the LED blinks every second".
- Motor learning: practice slowly first (metronome, then speed up step by step), split into chunks, mix exercises (interleaving) once each one is known, and record yourself to compare.

## Verification

- Values that can be calculated (resistors, intervals, tempos, quantities) are recalculated.
- Circuits: simulate when possible (point to a free simulator, checked to exist) and give expected measurements.
- Anything the reader reproduces is text: chords, tablature, rhythm patterns, wiring lists, recipes.

## Visuals

Chord diagrams, the fretboard with notes, tablature, rhythm patterns, circuit schematics, breadboard layouts, pinouts, step-by-step sequences. Real images only for what something looks like (an instrument, a component), with credits.

## Safety (mandatory when there is risk)

Before the first hands-on step, a `⚠️ Safety` box: mains voltage, lithium batteries, soldering, tools, heat, posture and strain injuries. Short, specific and never skippable. Include the bill of materials with approximate cost when parts are needed.

## Cautions

- No song lyrics or protected scores: public-domain songs or exercises written for the book.
- The book has no audio or video: say how to hear or see the result (metronome, tuner, recording yourself) without inventing links.
