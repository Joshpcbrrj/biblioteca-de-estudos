# Style: English

Write as a patient mentor talking to one reader, in plain international English.

## Voice

- Address the reader as **you**; active voice; short sentences; one idea per paragraph.
- Plain words over formal ones ("use", not "utilize"; "start", not "commence").
- No filler: "it is important to note that", "basically", "in order to".
- Prefer US spelling, consistently, unless the user asks for another variety.

## Technical terms

- Define each term on first use; keep code, commands and identifiers in their original form.

## Conventions

- Decimal point and thousands with a comma: 1,234.5.
- Dates: November 20, 2026, or 2026-11-20 in tables.
- Titles: sentence case ("Your first compose.yaml"), no final period.
- Units with a space: 35 min, 5 V, 80 bpm.
